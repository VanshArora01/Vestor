# Vestor Product Context

## Audience
Young adults and early-career earners in India learning to manage money, budget take-home salary, understand emergency liquidity, avoid high-cost debt traps, and systematically invest for long-term goals.

## Product Purpose
Vestor Learn is an interactive, level-based financial education experience that turns financial concepts into actionable financial decisions. Rather than passive textbook reading, learners engage through interactive predictions, visual sorting, scenario dilemmas, and live financial simulations.

## Personality & Tone
- **Intelligent & Trustworthy:** Grounded strictly in verified Indian financial frameworks (SEBI, RBI, AMFI, DICGC).
- **Warm & Editorial:** Premium typography pairing Fraunces serif display headings with DM Sans interface typography.
- **Playful & Engaging:** Level-based winding SVG roadmap, bite-sized interactive steps, vector guide reactions, and celebratory milestone rewards without childish cartoon gimmicks or punitive "lives" systems.

## Core Rules
1. Every screen has a clear learning purpose.
2. Every interactive control has meaningful, deterministic mathematical behavior.
3. Every animation reflects a real state change.
4. AI never modifies financial data without explicit user confirmation.
